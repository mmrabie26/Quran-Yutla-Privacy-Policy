Privacy Policy for Quran Yutla

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Quran Yutlaa ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Quran Yutlaa collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our mobile application.

By using Quran Yutlaa, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to provide the app's learning, recitation, account, and subscription features.

### Account Information

When you create or use an account, we may collect:

- Full name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Password, stored securely by our authentication system
- User role, such as student, teacher, or parent
- Country
- Age group
- Gender
- Student codes or linked child/student accounts
- Number of children, for parent accounts
- Notification or device identifier, when used for app notifications

### Quran Learning and Recitation Information

When you use recitation features, we may collect:

- Audio recordings that you record or upload for recitation review
- Selected Surah and Ayah range
- Notes you choose to submit
- Recitation history
- Teacher evaluations, scores, comments, and feedback
- Progress and learning activity connected to your account

### Payment and Subscription Information

If you purchase or subscribe to paid plans, we may collect information needed to start and manage the payment process, such as the selected plan. Payment processing may be handled by third-party payment providers. We do not intend to store full payment card numbers in the app.

### Technical and Usage Information

We may collect technical information needed to run, secure, debug, and improve the app, including:

- App version
- Device and operating system information
- Crash reports and diagnostic logs
- Network request information
- Feature usage and performance information

## 2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

- Create, authenticate, and manage user accounts
- Provide Quran reading, listening, recitation, and evaluation features
- Connect students with teachers or parents where applicable
- Show recitation history, reports, and progress
- Process subscriptions or payment-related actions
- Send important account, learning, or app notifications
- Improve app reliability, performance, and security
- Detect, prevent, and respond to misuse, fraud, or technical issues
- Comply with legal obligations

## 3. App Permissions

Quran Yutlaa may request the following device permissions:

- Microphone: to record Quran recitations when you choose to use recording features.
- Notifications: to send reminders, account updates, learning updates, or other app-related notifications.
- Internet access: to connect to our servers, load Quran content, submit recitations, sign in, and use online features.
- Audio settings and wake lock: to support stable audio playback and recording behavior.
- File access through file picker: to allow you to select audio files for upload when you choose to do so.

You can manage app permissions from your device settings. Some features may not work if required permissions are disabled.

## 4. Audio Recordings

Audio recordings are collected only when you record or upload recitations through the app. These recordings may be uploaded to our servers so they can be reviewed, analyzed, stored in your recitation history, or evaluated by teachers where applicable.

Do not upload audio that contains private information unrelated to Quran recitation.

## 5. Children and Parent Accounts

Quran Yutlaa may be used by students, parents, and teachers. Parent accounts may link to child or student accounts to view learning progress and recitation activity.

If the app is used by children, parents or legal guardians should supervise the child's use of the app. We collect child/student information only as needed to provide learning, recitation, account-linking, progress, and teacher/parent review features.

Parents or legal guardians may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of their child's information.

## 6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information.

We may share information only in the following situations:

- With service providers that help us operate the app, such as hosting, analytics, crash reporting, notifications, payment processing, or infrastructure providers.
- With teachers, students, or parents connected through the app, only as needed for learning, recitation review, progress reports, and account-linked features.
- When required by law, regulation, legal process, or government request.
- To protect the safety, rights, property, or security of users, Quran Yutlaa, or others.

## 7. Third-Party Services

Quran Yutlaa may use third-party services for features such as:

- Backend hosting and API services
- Crash reporting and diagnostics, including Sentry
- Payment processing
- Push notifications
- Audio processing, storage, or delivery

These third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies and only as needed to provide their services.

## 8. Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect user information. Sensitive account tokens may be stored on your device using secure storage. Local app preferences, such as language and theme settings, may be stored on your device.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed to provide the app, maintain accounts, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and protect the app from misuse.

Audio recordings, recitation history, evaluations, and account information may remain stored while your account is active unless deletion is requested or required by law.
